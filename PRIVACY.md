# Mono Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Mono is an Android home-screen launcher. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not send any personal data off your phone. The app has no internet permission of its own.

## What Mono reads, and why

Everything listed here is used only on your phone. None of it is uploaded, sold or shared.

| Data | Why Mono uses it |
| --- | --- |
| **Installed apps** | To list, search and open your apps, and to show their icons and shortcuts. |
| **Notifications** (optional, only if you turn on notification access) | To show the latest notification under each favorite app on the home screen and let you open or dismiss it. Notification content is held in memory and never stored or sent anywhere. |
| **Calendar** (optional, only if you grant calendar access) | To show today's next event under the clock. |
| **Accessibility service** (optional, only if you turn it on) | Used only to lock the screen when you double-tap the home screen and to open the notification shade. It subscribes to no accessibility events and cannot read the content of your screen. |
| **System settings** (optional) | To change brightness, ringer, Do Not Disturb, torch and volume from Mono's quick controls, only when you use them. |
| **Wallpaper** | To set one of the bundled wallpapers when you choose it. |

Your settings (favorites, hidden apps, renamed apps, usage counts used for sorting search results, and so on) are stored privately on your phone. They are excluded from Google cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

## Exporting settings

If you use **Settings → Export settings**, Mono writes a backup file to the location you choose. Mono does not upload that file anywhere. Who can see it depends on where you save it.

## Fonts

If you pick one of the optional Google Fonts, Android downloads it through Google Play services. Mono only requests the font by name and sends no personal data with that request. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

Mono is not directed at children under 13.
